About Our Pool

Aqua Ravi Swimming Pool

A modern, family-friendly aquatic facility designed to deliver the ultimate swimming experience in a clean, safe, and peaceful environment. Whether you're a professional swimmer, a fitness enthusiast, or seeking a refreshing escape — Aqua Ravi is your perfect space.

The pool features an Olympic-size layout with dedicated sections for beginners, children, and advanced swimmers. Professional lifeguards and trainers are always on duty, alongside clean changing rooms, secure lockers, and a fully filtered water system.

Our Vision

Marabi Group

At Marabi Group, our mission is to bring healthy, accessible, and enjoyable swimming experiences to people across Pakistan. We believe that swimming is not just a sport, but a lifestyle that promotes health, fitness, discipline, and joy.

Through the Aqua Ravi project and future developments, Marabi Group aims to scatter world-class swimming pools across regions — making them part of every community's recreational and fitness culture. Our vision is to raise awareness about water safety, swimming education, and healthy lifestyles, especially among children and youth.

We're not just building pools — we're building a swimming culture. 🌊🏊‍♂️
